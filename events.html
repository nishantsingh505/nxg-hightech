Tech Forum 2024: "Innovation with NX & Data Management with Teamcenter"

We extend our gratitude to all esteemed participants from automotive OEMs, Tier-1 die & mold suppliers, and consumer durable manufacturers for attending the Siemens PLM-NX: Tech Forum India organized by NXG Hightech. Here are key insights from this year's technical presentations:
